# Answer shape

Lead with the mechanism in one sentence the creator could say out loud about their own work. Then, per mechanism:

- **Belief shift and identity reward.** What the viewer believed before and after; who they get to be.
- **Recipe.** Open, turn, close, in the creator's own vocabulary, drawn from the strand's evidence.
- **Proof.** Two or three evidence posts as hook · multiple · platform, with the ref so the user can open them.
- **Repeatability.** Attempts, hit rate and whether the last hit is recent; say plainly when a mechanism is fading.
- **Misses.** When the strand carries misses, one line on what the misses lacked.

Close with what to do next only when the user asked for advice. Otherwise stop at the evidence.
