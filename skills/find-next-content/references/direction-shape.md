# Direction shape

Each direction is four lines:

1. **Mechanism.** The strand name and its belief shift, in the creator's words.
2. **New surface.** The topic, format or platform that has not carried this mechanism yet.
3. **Descends from.** One evidence post as hook · multiple · ref.
4. **Why now.** Fresh demand, a fading strand worth reviving, or a thin idea with headroom; and the saturation warning if there is one.
