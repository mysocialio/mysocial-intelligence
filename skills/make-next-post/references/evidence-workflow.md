# Evidence Workflow

## Internal evidence first

Compare candidates across six dimensions:

1. Audience demand: comments, demand gaps, and audience-need paths.
2. Observed precedent: connections to posts that materially outperformed the creator's baseline on the relevant metric. The creative mechanism remains a hypothesis.
3. Contrast: similar posts that missed, including format or angle differences.
4. Novelty: nearest hook and claim similarity, not merely topic overlap.
5. Timing: recent territory momentum, fatigue, saturation, and newest relevant evidence.
6. Platform fit: format and performance history on the selected platform.

Generate five candidates that vary the underlying angle or mechanism, not only wording. Select privately and explain the winner with traceable evidence paths.

## Build the working brief

Follow the creative-brief fields in the main skill before drafting. Preserve source excerpts alongside interpretations so a later model can check the actual argument. Keep one primary mechanism per candidate, a few distinct precedents, the most relevant counterexample and the necessary creator facts. Drop redundant labels and duplicate memories before dropping evidence or a material limitation. This is a task-local brief, not another stored analysis or a requirement to send theory books to the model.

For a post praising restraint **when options exist**, transfer the options → voluntary choice → selectivity relationship. Merely praising two admirable traits loses its mechanism. For a folding tutorial, transfer the visible starting state → reproducible steps → useful result; do not invent a status identity or emotional wound. Source claims remain attributed until independently supported.

Use available comments to test the interpretation: keep disagreement, misunderstood comparisons and requests the creator cannot fulfill. One person's expressed reaction is not the entire audience's motive. Check the comment sample and classification denominator; neither likes nor comment volume tells you whether viewers understood or agreed. If feedback reveals an unavailable offer or a product problem, the brief may require action on that problem before another claim.

For metrics, retain the returned platform, channel/format cohort, denominator and time window. Reposts of one idea are not independent tests of generality. A high-view precedent with weak response on the requested objective can be a counterexample. Missing comparison data means uncertainty, not evidence that the mechanism always works.

For the final observable test, name a measurable outcome and a valid comparison: for example saves per reported reach against comparable recent posts, only if those inputs exist. If the objective is understanding, look for relevant expressed understanding or a direct feedback question; views alone cannot measure it. Do not invent a numerical target or imply a causal test where only observational comparisons are available.

## Conditional external research

Research externally when any condition is true:

- The user asks about what is current, trending, or happening now.
- Fewer than five relevant internal posts exist.
- The newest relevant hit is more than 45 days old.
- The tool returns `coverage.status = "insufficient"`.

Search the selected platform first. Prefer normalized outliers over raw high-view accounts. Transcribe no more than two videos, then use their mechanics as evidence rather than copying their ideas or wording.

If both internal and external evidence remain empty, produce a best-effort package, label the evidence limitation, list it under uncertainties, and do not invent metrics or citations.

## Preflight

Re-check the completed hook and angle. If the nearest hook similarity is at least `0.95`, change the angle or promise—not just synonyms. If evidence conflicts, state the uncertainty instead of inventing confidence.
